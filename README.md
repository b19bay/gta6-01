Welcome to Costa Vela, a tropical coastal city where you step into the shoes of Dario Cruz and make your way in its criminal underworld. The whole game ships as plain static files, so it plays offline with no build step and nothing to install. Mara Solis sets you a chain of 6 story missions to work through, and between jobs you can steal cars, get into fights, and chase money around the city while trying to shake the law whenever the wanted level climbs from 1 to 5 stars. A HUD with a minimap keeps you oriented, your progress is kept in localStorage, and every sound is synthesized live with WebAudio.

## Run it

Just open `game/index.html` in a desktop Chrome window. Everything the game needs is already sitting in that folder, so it starts straight from your local files.

## Layout

- `game/` — where the playable game lives (`index.html` plus the code under `src/`)
- `screenshots/` — captures taken from the game while it was actually running
- `main.ts` — the entry script that gathers the game and the screenshots into the output dossier
